1. About

Weather Presenter AI ("we") is an automated weather forecast show. Every episode (script, presenter video, voice and graphics) is generated by artificial intelligence and published to our own channels on YouTube and Facebook.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or any other users. We do not use cookies, tracking, analytics SDKs or advertising identifiers on this website or in our tool.

3. Use of YouTube API Services

Our internal publishing tool uses the YouTube API Services only to upload our own videos and Shorts, update their metadata and run scheduled live broadcasts on our own YouTube channel, and to read basic statistics (view counts) of our own videos. It does not access, read or store data of other users or channels.

By watching our content on YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube is a Google service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Authorization data (OAuth)

The only account that authorizes our tool is the channel owner's own Google account. The OAuth access/refresh token is stored only on our own server in a file readable solely by the operator's account, and is used solely for the actions listed in section 3. It is never shared with third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation we delete the stored token within 7 days.

5. Facebook

We use the Facebook Graph API only to publish our own videos and live broadcasts to Facebook Pages we manage. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

6. Weather data

Forecasts are based on public data from Open-Meteo.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep only the IDs and URLs of our own published videos (to avoid duplicate uploads) and aggregate view counts of our own videos. Because we do not store personal data, there is nothing personal to delete. If you believe we hold any information about you, email us and we will respond and delete it within 30 days.
