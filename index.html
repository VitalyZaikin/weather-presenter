What is this?

Weather Presenter AI is a short (~60 s) daily weather forecast in Ukrainian for Kyiv, Odesa, Lviv, Kharkiv, Donetsk and Crimea. Every episode — script, AI presenter video, synthetic voice and map graphics — is generated automatically by artificial intelligence from public forecast data (Open-Meteo), without human involvement, and is clearly labelled as AI-generated.

Watch on YouTube

How it works

A private, server-side tool owned by the channel operator prepares the episode each morning and publishes it to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services (videos, Shorts and short scheduled live broadcasts), and to our own Facebook pages. The tool acts only on our own channel and pages. It has no other users and does not access or store data of viewers or other channels.
